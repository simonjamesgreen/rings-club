Privacy policy

The short version: Grudge uses your activity to run your league, shows it to the people in your leagues, and nothing else. No ads, no tracking, no selling your data.

Last updated 9 November 2026

Who we are

Grudge is made by Simon Green, an independent developer based in London, UK. For this policy, Simon Green is the data controller. Contact: simonjamesgreen@gmail.com.

Who can see it

People in your leagues see your name, colour, your daily active calories and target, your percentage of target, points, cards and your messages. They also see your season totals for the extra stats (distances, stairs, walking pace and stride) unless you switch off Share extra stats in You → Account. Shields are only visible to you. Private messages are only visible to you and the person you are chatting with, unless one of you reports a message, which sends it to us to review. Nobody outside your leagues can see your activity.

Apple Health data

Data from Apple Health is only used to run your leagues. It is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with anyone for any other purpose. You can stop Grudge reading it at any time in the Health app under Sharing → Apps → Grudge.

The Pundit (optional)

If the host of a league switches on the Pundit, Grudge writes a daily match report using Anthropic's Claude AI. For that report we send Anthropic the league name, players' names, each player's percentage of target, points, rest days, and the cards played that day. We never send calorie figures or any other raw Apple Health data. If the host also allows the Pundit to read Banter, recent chat messages from that day are included. Anthropic processes this to write the report and, under its commercial terms, does not use it to train its models. Banter tells everyone in the league when the Pundit is switched on or off.

Services we use

  • Supabase: hosts our database and server code, in London (UK).
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, Apple Health on your device, in-app purchases and push notifications.
  • Anthropic: only for the Pundit, as described above.

We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

Legal basis (UK GDPR)

We use your data to provide the service you signed up for (contract). Activity data from Apple Health is health data, so we only read it with your explicit permission, which you give in Apple's Health permission screen and can withdraw at any time.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. When you delete your account (You → Account → Delete account), your profile, activity, scores, cards, messages and push tokens are deleted straight away. Leagues you host pass to another member, or are deleted if empty. Feedback you sent is kept without your name. Deleted data clears from our backups within 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Email simonjamesgreen@gmail.com and we'll respond within a month. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

Grudge is not for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app if anything important changes.
