Common questions

Where do the verses come from? Every verse is shown word for word from the Berean Standard Bible. Manna’s AI only chooses which reviewed passage fits what you shared. It never writes or changes Scripture.

I didn’t get my morning verse. Check Settings → Notifications → Manna on your iPhone and make sure notifications are allowed. Then open Manna once, so it can schedule the next week of reminders.

Can I move my journal to a new phone? Not yet. In this test version your journal is kept only on your phone. Moving it between phones is planned.

How do I delete a stone, or everything? Open the stone in your Ebenezer journal and tap Remove this stone. To remove everything, delete the Manna app; all your data goes with it.

Is Manna a replacement for pastoral care or counselling? No. Manna offers Scripture, not advice. If you or someone you know is struggling, please talk to someone you trust. In the UK you can call Samaritans free on 116 123, any time, or text SHOUT to 85258. In an emergency, call 999.
