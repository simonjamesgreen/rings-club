Your mates. Your Watch. One grudge.

Grudge is a fitness league for small groups of friends. Everyone races against their own normal day, so the fittest person doesn't win by default. Hit your target, earn cards, and fire them at each other.

Private leaguesFair targetsCards and chaosBanter

How it works

  • Grudge reads your active calories from Apple Health and works out your normal day.
  • Each day you're scored on how far past your target you get, as a percentage.
  • Hit your target and you earn cards: Darts, Heat-seekers, Turbos and Shields.
  • Points every morning, a winner every season, and someone always holds a grudge.

Grudge needs an iPhone and works best with an Apple Watch.
