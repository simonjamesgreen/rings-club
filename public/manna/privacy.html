Privacy policy

The short version: your journal stays on your phone. What you type is sent only to find your verse, and Manna doesn’t keep it. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

Last updated 4 November 2026

Who we are

Manna: Daily Bread is made by Simon Green, an independent developer based in London, UK. For this policy, Simon Green is the data controller. Contact: simonjamesgreen@gmail.com.

What is sent to find your verse

When you check in, the words you type are sent over an encrypted connection to Manna’s server, which is hosted by Supabase in the European Union. To choose a passage, the server passes your words to Anthropic’s Claude AI, together with Manna’s list of reviewed passages. The server then reads the chosen verse from Manna’s Bible database and sends it back to your phone.

When you read the Bible, search it, or open a topic, Advent day or reading plan, your phone asks Manna’s server for that passage. These requests contain only the passage or search words, not anything from your journal or prayer list.

  • Manna does not save what you type, and doesn’t link it to you. There are no accounts.
  • Anthropic processes your words under its commercial terms. It does not use them to train its models, and may keep them for a limited period for safety and abuse monitoring. See Anthropic’s privacy policy.
  • Like any website or app server, our hosting providers keep short-lived technical logs, such as IP addresses, to keep the service running and secure.
  • Please don’t include anything you wouldn’t want sent to a server, such as full names, addresses or medical details.

Sharing

When you share a verse, the picture card is made on your phone and goes only where you choose to send it.

What we don’t do

No advertising, no analytics or tracking, no selling or sharing your data, and no access to your contacts, photos or location.

Safety

If something you type suggests you or someone else may be at risk, Manna shows ways to get help from real people, such as Samaritans and 999. This check happens automatically and nothing about it is stored.

Children

Manna is meant for people aged 13 and over.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Because your journal stays on your phone, you control it directly. For anything else, email simonjamesgreen@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
