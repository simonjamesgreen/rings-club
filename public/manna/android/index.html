How to install

  1. Open this page on your Android phone and tap Download Manna for Android.
  2. When it has downloaded, tap the file (or open it from Downloads).
  3. If your phone says apps from this source are not allowed, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Tap Install. If Google Play Protect asks, tap Install anyway. It warns about any app that is not from the Play Store yet.
  5. Open Manna. When it asks to send notifications, tap Allow if you want reminders.
Updates do not arrive automatically in this test version. When there is a new one, open this page again and install it over the top. Your journal and prayer list stay on your phone.
