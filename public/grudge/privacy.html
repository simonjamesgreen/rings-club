Grudge privacy policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

Grudge is a fitness league game for friends. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. In short: we use your activity totals to run the game, your league can see them, and we never sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who we are

Grudge is made by Simon James Green, an individual developer based in London, United Kingdom, who is the data controller for the app. You can contact us at simonjamesgreen@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Sign in with Apple: a unique account ID from Apple and the email address Apple shares (this can be a private relay address if you choose "Hide My Email"). If you let Apple share your first name, we use it to suggest your display name.
  • Your profile: the display name and colour you choose.
  • Activity from Apple Health: with your permission, daily totals of active calories, steps and exercise minutes for roughly the last five weeks, kept up to date while you use the app. We read daily totals only. We do not read workouts, heart rate, location, sleep or any other health data, and we never write anything to Apple Health.
  • Game data: leagues you create or join, seasons, scores, power-up cards and rest days.
  • Messages: chat messages you post in a league's banter feed, plus any reports or blocks you make.
  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, a device token so we can send you banter and game alerts. You can turn these off in the app or in iPhone Settings.

We do not use advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

How we use it

  • To work out your "normal" day and score each day of the game against it.
  • To show your league its live race, results, cards and banter.
  • To keep the game fair and safe, including handling reports of abusive messages.

The legal basis for this under UK GDPR is performance of our agreement with you (running the game you signed up for), and your explicit consent for the activity data you allow the app to read from Apple Health. You can withdraw that consent at any time in the iPhone Settings app under Health, then Data Access & Devices, then Grudge.

Who can see it

  • People in your leagues can see your display name, colour, daily activity totals, scores, played cards, rest days and messages. People outside your leagues cannot.
  • Service providers that host the app's data for us: Supabase (database and accounts, stored in London, UK) and Expo (app updates and delivering notifications, which do not include your health data). They process data only on our instructions.

We never sell your data, and data from Apple Health is never used for advertising or shared with data brokers.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. If you delete your account, your profile, activity, messages and game data are deleted. If you host a league, it passes to another member; leagues with nobody left in them are removed.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to the You tab and tap Delete account. You can also email us and we will delete it for you.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, object to or restrict how we use it, and ask for it in a portable format. Email us to make a request. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

Grudge is not intended for anyone under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
