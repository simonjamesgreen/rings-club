Common questions

My calories aren't showing. Open the Health app, go to Sharing → Apps → Grudge and check Active Energy is switched on. Then open Grudge so it can sync. Your Watch needs to have synced to your iPhone first.

Yesterday's score looks low. Each day is scored the next morning at your league's scoring time. If you didn't open Grudge between midnight and then, calories your Watch synced late may not have been counted. Grudge sends a nudge 30 minutes before.

How is my target set? From your normal active calories over the last few weeks. The host of a private league can adjust targets before a season starts.

I bought a drop and nothing happened. Check your connection and reopen the app; drops are delivered once Apple confirms the purchase. If it still hasn't arrived, email me with the date and league name.

How do I report or block someone? Press and hold their message in Banter and choose Report or Block. You can unblock people in You → Account.

How do I delete my account? In the app, go to You → Account → Delete account. This removes your profile, activity and messages. See the privacy policy for details.
