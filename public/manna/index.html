Daily bread for whatever today brings.

Tell Manna what today holds and it finds you a Bible verse that fits, word for word from a real translation, with the passage around it so it’s never out of context.

“Because of the loving devotion of the LORD we are not consumed, for His mercies never fail. They are new every morning; great is Your faithfulness!”

Lamentations 3:22–23 · Berean Standard Bible

What Manna does

  • Finds a verse for what you’re going through, or for someone you’re praying for.
  • Shows every verse in context, with a short note on why it fits.
  • Follows up on what you shared and keeps an Ebenezer journal of how God helped.
  • Lets you share a verse as a picture card.

Manna is in testing and coming soon to the App Store.
